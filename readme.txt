Lore:
    Your a young child who watched the Artemis II launch and you wish to launch your own rocket ship to hopefully reach the moon
    before billionaires and want to improve space exploration so you gain funding from people wanting to see you succeed.

Gameplay:
    You launch your own rocket called Hopes and Dreams(number based on failed attempts) and attempt to reach the moon, after every attempt
    you gain money which you can put towards upgrades. These upgrades improve fuel capacity, rocket strength, rocket durability.
